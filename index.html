How to play

Level the beam

Fixed anchors tip the beam. Build words from the rack and place them on an arm until the balance is within ±1.

Torque = letters × notch

A word lands on the notch of its heaviest letter: the small number on each tile. Further out pushes harder.

Spend heavy letters wisely

Each tile is used once and the rack never refills. A heavy letter only counts once per word, so spread them out.

Beat par

3 to 5 lettersAny dictionary word, once per day. A word that isn't accepted costs nothing.
ParThe fewest words that can balance today's beam, proven by the solver. Hit it for Gold; one more is Solved.
Undo is freeTake back any word and get its tiles back. It never counts as a move.
HintFirst checks whether par is still in reach, then nudges, then reveals a word. Built a word? Hint checks it before you place it. Each step adds 1 to your leaderboard score.
